## Client Feedback
David said he really liked the website I created, but requested two changes:

1. He is developing a waitlist that customers can sign up for. Once accepted, they will be able to see secret menu items and upcoming drinks. He asked me to include this on the website, preferably on the About page.
2. Some images on the Menu page did not look right on mobile. He asked me to fix their layout.

These points summarize our conversation rather than quote his exact words.

## Requested Changes
- Add information about the secret menu waitlist to the About page.
- Fix the Menu page images on smaller screens.

## AI Options and My Decision
My AI agent suggested linking to David’s existing signup page, building a custom signup form, or adding a coming-soon section; I chose the existing signup link because David already has a working waitlist service.

## Changes Made
- Added a Secret Menu Waitlist section to the About page with a button linking to David’s signup page.
- Fixed the mobile Menu layout so images appear below the text, centered and contained within their cards.
- Tested the waitlist button and checked the Menu page in a narrow browser window.
