# Mini Project 1 Proposal

## Client Brief

**Client:** Matcha by Chae, a matcha business founded by David, a Babson College student. The business now has a physical location in Boston.

**Purpose:** The purpose of the website is to introduce people to Matcha by Chae, show what the business offers, and make it easy for customers to find information about the menu, location, hours, and brand.

**Audience:** The main audience is college students, young professionals, matcha lovers, and people in Boston who are looking for a good matcha spot. Many customers will probably access the website from their phones when looking for the menu or store information.

**Key Action:** The main action I want visitors to take is to view the menu and then visit the Matcha by Chae store.


## Pages Needed

- **Home:** An introduction to Matcha by Chae with photos, featured drinks, and important information.
- **About:** The story behind Matcha by Chae and what makes the brand unique.
- **Menu:** A clear presentation of the drinks and other available products.
- **Visit / Contact:** The store location, hours, contact information, and social media links.

## Style Preferences

David described the Matcha by Chae brand as modern, fun, welcoming, youthful, and focused on high-quality matcha.

The website should be clean and minimal and should match the existing Matcha by Chae branding. I will use the existing logo, colors, photos, and other brand elements as the basis for the design.

The website should be visually focused and should not contain too much text. David does not want the website to feel cluttered, overly corporate, or difficult to navigate.

The website will be designed for both desktop and mobile, but mobile is especially important because many customers will probably search for the store or menu on their phones.


## Inspiration Sites

During the interview, we looked at George Howell Coffee, Blank Street Coffee, and Greystone Coffee as inspiration for the Matcha by Chae website.

- **George Howell Coffee:** We liked the clean and professional layout, strong product photography, and the way the website makes the products an important part of the design.

- **Blank Street Coffee:** We liked the modern and youthful feel and the strong brand identity. The website is simple to navigate and visually focused without having too much text.

- **Greystone Coffee:** We liked the welcoming café feeling, simple design, and use of photography to communicate the atmosphere of the brand.

These websites helped establish the direction for Matcha by Chae: a website that is clean, modern, visual, easy to navigate, and consistent with the personality of the brand.


## Layout Plan

The website will use the same navigation bar on every page. The navigation will include the Matcha by Chae logo and links to Home, About, Menu, and Visit.

The **Home page** will begin with a large hero image featuring Matcha by Chae. It will include the brand name, a short introduction, and a button that takes visitors directly to the menu. Below the hero section, there will be a short introduction to the brand, featured drinks, a preview of the Matcha by Chae story, and store location and hours.

The **About page** will explain the story behind Matcha by Chae, how the business started, and what makes the brand unique.

The **Menu page** will display Matcha by Chae's drinks and products in a simple and visual layout. The menu should be easy to read on both desktop and mobile devices.

The **Visit / Contact page** will include the store address, hours, contact information, and social media links so customers can easily find and connect with the business.

The footer will appear consistently across all four pages and include basic business information and social media links.
