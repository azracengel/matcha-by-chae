# Mini Project 1 Proposal

## Client

My client is Matcha by Chae, a matcha business founded by David, a Babson College student. The business now has a physical location in Boston.

## Project Overview

For this project, I will create a website for Matcha by Chae. The goal of the website is to introduce customers to the brand and make it easy for them to find important information about the business, including the menu, location, hours, and story behind Matcha by Chae.

The website should reflect the personality of the brand while remaining simple and easy to navigate.

## Target Audience

The main target audience includes college students, young professionals, matcha lovers, and people in Boston who are looking for a matcha spot.

Because many customers are likely to search for the business from their phones, the website should work well on both desktop and mobile devices.

## Website Goals

The website should allow visitors to:

- Learn about Matcha by Chae
- View the menu
- Find the store location and hours
- Learn more about the story behind the business
- See photos of the drinks and store
- Find the business's social media

The main goal is to encourage visitors to learn about the brand and visit the physical store.

## Proposed Pages

The website will include at least four main pages:

1. **Home** – An introduction to Matcha by Chae with photos and important information.
2. **About** – The story behind Matcha by Chae and the business.
3. **Menu** – A clear presentation of the drinks and other available products.
4. **Visit / Contact** – Store location, hours, contact information, and social media.

## Design Direction

The website will have a modern, clean, and minimal design that reflects the existing Matcha by Chae branding. The overall feeling should be youthful, welcoming, and visually focused.

The design will use the business's existing colors, logo, photos, and other brand elements when available. The website should avoid looking cluttered or overly corporate.

## Content and Assets

The website can use existing Matcha by Chae materials such as:

- Logo and branding
- Menu information
- Photos of drinks
- Photos of the store
- Business information
- Social media links

## Success Criteria

The project will be successful if visitors can quickly understand what Matcha by Chae is, view the menu, learn about the brand, and easily find the information they need to visit the store.

The final website should also be responsive, easy to navigate, and consistent with the Matcha by Chae brand.
